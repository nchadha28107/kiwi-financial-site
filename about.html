Our mission

Make borrowing in New Zealand feel less like a test you might fail.

Most people only arrange a mortgage a handful of times in their life. The banks do it ten thousand times a day. That asymmetry is why good advice matters — and why we explain everything in plain English, show you the trade-offs, and never let a client sign something they do not fully understand.

Independence

Why “100% independent” is more than a slogan

A bank employee can only offer you that bank’s products at that bank’s criteria. If their servicing calculator says no, the conversation is over. We are not bound to any lender, so a decline from one is simply the start of the next application.

We are paid a commission by whichever lender you choose, and that commission does not change your interest rate. We disclose exactly what we receive. If a lender ever paid more to push a worse outcome, we would still not recommend it — our business runs on referrals, not one-off deals.

    Registration & regulation

    Kiwi Financial Solutions is a registered Financial Advice Provider under the Financial Markets Conduct Act 2013 and is listed on the Financial Service Providers Register (FSPR). We are bound by the Code of Professional Conduct for Financial Advice Services, which requires us to give priority to your interests, exercise care, diligence and skill, and ensure you understand the advice you receive.

    All lending we arrange is subject to lender criteria and to responsible lending obligations under the Credit Contracts and Consumer Finance Act 2003 (CCCFA). We are also a member of an approved dispute resolution scheme, which is free for you to use if you are ever unhappy with our service.
