What is asset finance and who is it for?

Asset finance is lending secured against the asset itself rather than against your house. Because the asset provides the security, approvals are faster and you avoid tying up equity in your home.

It suits tradies buying a ute or trailer, transport operators adding to a fleet, contractors acquiring plant, and businesses upgrading production equipment. Terms generally run one to five years and the structure can be matched to the asset’s useful life.
