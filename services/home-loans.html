What is a home loan and who is it for?

A home loan is lending secured against a residential property you intend to live in. In New Zealand most owner-occupiers borrow up to 80% of the property value, with the balance coming from savings, KiwiSaver, equity or a family contribution.

We work with first home buyers, people moving up, people downsizing, and anyone who has been told “no” by their own bank. Because we are not owned by a lender, we can go straight to the banks most likely to say yes to your situation rather than putting you through the same declined application twice.

Eligibility

What lenders will want to see

Nothing here is a hard rule — every lender weighs these differently, and part of our job is knowing which ones are flexible on which points.

Check my eligibility free
  • ✓A deposit of at least 5% (First Home Loan) or 10–20% for most standard lending
  • ✓Stable income — PAYE, self-employed with two years of accounts, or contract income
  • ✓A clean-ish credit history (small blemishes are usually workable)
  • ✓NZ citizen, permanent resident, resident visa, or in many cases a work visa
  • ✓Enough surplus income to pass the lender’s servicing test at their stressed test rate
