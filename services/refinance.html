What is refinancing and who is it for?

Refixing means agreeing a new fixed rate with your existing lender. Refinancing means moving the loan to a different lender, usually to get a better rate, release equity, consolidate debt or restructure the loan entirely.

If your fixed term is expiring in the next six months, or you have not reviewed your mortgage in two years, you are almost certainly leaving money on the table. Most lenders also pay a cash contribution for new business, which often more than covers your break and legal costs.

Key benefits

What you get working with us

We negotiate, you do not have to

We go back to your bank with competing offers in hand. Often they will match rather than lose you.

Cash contributions

Lenders commonly offer around 0.6–0.9% of the loan to win your business. We make sure that is on the table.

Restructure while you are at it

Rolling expiry dates, splitting across terms and adding a revolving credit facility can matter more than the headline rate.

Debt consolidation done properly

Rolling short-term debt into a 30-year mortgage can cost you. We only recommend it when the numbers genuinely work.
