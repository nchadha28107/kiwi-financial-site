Why the lowest rate isn’t always the best deal

Cash contributions

A lender 0.10% higher that pays you $6,000 cash is usually ahead for the first few years. We run both numbers before recommending.

Low-equity margins

Advertised specials assume 20% equity. Under that, most lenders add a margin that is not shown on any rate table.

Structure beats rate

Splitting terms, keeping a revolving facility and not resetting to 30 years will usually save more than chasing 0.05%.
