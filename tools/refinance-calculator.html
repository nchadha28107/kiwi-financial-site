Before you switch

Three things that change the answer

Cash contributions

Lenders commonly offer around 0.6–0.9% of the loan as a cash contribution for new business. On a $620k loan that can more than cover your break fees.

Clawback periods

Cash contributions usually come with a 3–4 year clawback. If you might sell soon, the maths changes. We’ll flag it before you commit.

Don’t reset to 30 years

Refinancing back to a fresh 30-year term lowers your payment but can cost more overall. Keeping your existing end date is usually the smarter move.
