Good to know

Getting more out of the numbers

Fortnightly beats monthly

Twenty-six fortnightly payments equal thirteen monthly payments a year, not twelve. That extra payment can cut years off a 30-year loan without you feeling it.

LVR drives your rate

Above 80% LVR most lenders add a low-equity margin. Getting your deposit over 20% — or using a First Home Loan — can materially reduce what you pay.

Split your loan

Most Kiwis fix part of the loan and float part of it. We structure splits so you can make lump-sum repayments without break costs.
